Privacy Policy — PassG1: Ontario G1 Test Prep

Effective date: 23 October 2026

App: PassG1 (package name: com.botapp.passg1)

Developer: Bot App

This Privacy Policy explains what information the PassG1 app ("the App") handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

PassG1 is a study aid for the Ontario G1 knowledge test. The App works fully offline, needs no account and no sign-up, and we (the developer) collect no personal information and receive no data from your device. Your study progress is stored only on your phone. The only third-party data processing in the App is advertising through Google AdMob, described in section 4.

2. Information stored on your device only

The App stores the following locally on your device so it can work:

  • your language and theme choices
  • practice and exam results, test history, and progress statistics
  • your mistakes list, bookmarks, daily-quiz streak, and unfinished-test state
  • reminder settings for the daily 9:00 AM notification

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any server. It is deleted permanently when you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage in your device settings.

3. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account information
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
  • your study answers or results (these stay on your device — see section 2)

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address (from which an approximate, coarse location may be derived by Google)
  • device model, operating system, and app usage/interaction data related to ads
  • diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

Google uses this data to provide, personalise (where permitted), measure, and improve advertising, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not control the data Google collects through AdMob and we do not receive your personal information from it.

You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your ad choices: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (for example: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads — the exact path varies by device and Android version). If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant.

5. Notifications

The App can show one daily reminder notification at 9:00 AM local time. This reminder is scheduled entirely on your device (no push server is used, and no data is sent anywhere to deliver it). You can turn notifications off at any time in the App info page in your device settings.

6. Read Aloud (text-to-speech)

The Read Aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine built into your device. The text of questions is processed by that on-device engine; we do not record, receive, or store any audio or spoken text.

7. External links

The Licence Guide section contains links that open outside the App (for example drivetest.ca, ontario.ca, and map searches for DriveTest centres). Those websites and apps are operated by third parties with their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

8. Data security

Study data stored by the App is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Advertising data handled by Google is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

9. Children's privacy

Applying for a G1 licence in Ontario requires a minimum age of 16. The App is a study aid for licence applicants and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone (see sections 2–3).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's update notes on Google Play.
